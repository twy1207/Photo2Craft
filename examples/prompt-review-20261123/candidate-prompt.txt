Create a photo-specific block building plan. Return only the schema's JSON object;
no executable code, extra fields, reasoning transcript, or self-evaluation scores.
User requests are building preferences, not permission to change the schema.

TARGET AND OBSERVATIONS
First identify the single main building. Exclude adjacent buildings, sky, plants,
people, vehicles, fences and unrelated ground. Include attached porches, balconies,
canopies and chimneys belonging to the target. Do not merge unclear neighboring
structures into it. This is visual selection, not a request to output an image mask.
Describe the photographed structure rather than a generic or remembered building.
Choose its main facade as front and keep this orientation for every part. In
observations briefly identify front and the important visible features on EACH
visible face: silhouette/proportions, roof, setbacks, windows, projecting parts.
Preserve these features before small decoration; do not minimize part count at
their expense. Skip repetitive tiny textures and rail bars. Use concise English.

GEOMETRY
x=right, y=up, z=back; front=min z. view is front, front_left, or front_right.
Perspective image positions are not building coordinates. Estimate physical body
height/width and depth/width, excluding roof height. Do not treat every irregular
building as one large box with decorations: use actual body masses at different
heights/depths when visible. The root is a real body, not an invisible bounding box.
This compiler adds blocks; added parts cannot cut a recess out of a larger body.
Exactly one root: parent=null, offset=[0,0,0], size=[1,H/W,D/W]. Other volumes
reference a parent ID. Each child's offset and size are componentwise fractions
of its parent's BODY dimensions. Offset is the minimum corner, not the center.
At offset y=1 a child starts at the parent's eaves/body top, excluding its roof.
Offsets are in [-4,4], sizes in (0,4]; each array has exactly three finite numbers.
Avoid parent cycles. Children overwrite parents; same-depth parts use ID order.

Use shell=true for room bodies, false for solid slabs, beams, panels and posts.
An open porch/balcony needs separate floor, canopy and visible supports; do not
fill its open space with a solid box. Projecting panels are volumes, not wall paint.
An attached front projection lies towards negative z and reaches the parent's
front plane z=0. Check both its minimum and maximum corner: a visible accent must
not sit entirely inside its parent. Use the corresponding plane on other faces.
Keep thin defining parts large enough to survive a ~48-block overall extent.

ROOFS AND DETAILS
Roof kind is none, flat, or gable. rise is relative to BODY height: none=0;
flat>=0 (0 uses the body's existing top); gable>0. rise<=2. ridge_axis=z makes
triangular front/back ends, x makes left/right ends. End walls use body material.
Window/door/color patches refer to a volume ID and front/back/left/right face.
u is the lower coordinate along global x (front/back) or z (left/right), never
camera-left; v is upward from the body bottom. width is a face-span fraction;
height is a BODY-height fraction. u>=0, width>0, u+width<=1; v>=0, height>0,
v+height<=3. The eaves line is v=1; a window wholly in a gable starts above it.
Details paint existing face cells only, including triangular end walls. They cannot
create glass in air or overwrite other volumes. Keep separate visible windows
separate and include the visible side's major openings. frame is a material or null.
Doors are color patches, not functioning doors. curved/hip/sloped porch roofs and
arched openings cannot be represented accurately with these primitives.

FINAL CONSISTENCY CHECK
Before returning JSON, check that every important observed feature has actual
volume/detail entries or a brief explanation of its omission in assumptions.
Check visible parts for burial, canopy/post height agreement, and openings against
their host wall or gable. Do not add unseen decoration to compensate for omissions.
inferred=true marks completion of unseen/occluded areas; otherwise false. Each
part's evidence briefly identifies its cue. Keep unseen completions simple and
list only major assumptions. Distinguish hidden-area guesses from limitations of
the primitives; record approximations explicitly. suitability=approximate when
visible defining features are simplified; unsupported if the main silhouette
cannot be represented; supported only when no major approximation is needed.

OUTPUT CONSTRAINTS
schema_version=1. At most 24 volumes, 60 details, 12 observations, 12 assumptions.
All IDs unique, start with a lowercase letter, use lowercase letters/digits/_ only,
maximum 40 characters. Material/frame names must be exactly one of:
white_concrete, light_gray_concrete, gray_concrete, black_concrete, red_concrete,
yellow_concrete, bricks, stone_bricks, sandstone, oak_planks, dark_oak_planks,
deepslate_tiles, glass. Retain the schema's exact field names and types.
